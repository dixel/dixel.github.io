How I approach architecture

  1. Scope the problem.

    Understand what needs to work, for whom, and within which constraints.

  2. Draft a first solution.

    Start with an MVP that lets us test the assumptions against the actual problem.

  3. Decide what needs to last.

    Separate long-lived components from short-lived parts according to the problem and product lifecycle. Give them clear boundaries so they can change independently.

Revisit those boundaries as the product changes.
